# ADR 0010: Deliver One Trustworthy Workflow in Six to Eight Weeks

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-08

## Context

A single founder using coding agents could spread effort across the full platform vision and finish no credible workflow.

## Decision

Build one accounts-payable vertical slice in approximately six to eight weeks. Six weeks targets an internal alpha. Eight weeks targets a design-partner candidate. Optimize for maintainability, modularity, rapid iteration, and a runnable repository after every phase.

## Consequences

- Provenance, review, versioning, economics, isolation, and sandbox controls are not optional.
- Provider breadth, visualization polish, and advanced automation are cut first.
- Pilot, production, adoption, realized ROI, and pattern promotion remain later milestones.
