# Internal Alpha Delivery System

## What We're Building

Turn the staged delivery recommendation into an executable product and operating system. AI-FDE
will ship with three synthetic workflow profiles, durable internal-alpha assessments, objective
delivery and token-efficiency scorecards, a clean browser rehearsal covering every profile, and a
stricter production-readiness verifier.

Repository automation will prove code behavior. Human feedback and live cloud evidence will remain
explicit inputs; the system will not manufacture completed interviews, Auth0 sessions, AWS
deployments, Bedrock evaluations, or customer authorization.

## Why This Approach

Three approaches were considered:

1. **Documentation only.** Fast, but it would leave the one-workflow limitation and no durable
   measurement path.
2. **Deploy immediately.** It could exercise external services, but credentials, accountable
   operators, release identity, and customer authorization are absent. Treating those as implied
   would weaken the trust model.
3. **Build the controlled alpha system, then fail closed at external gates.** This produces all
   repository-controlled capability now and turns missing external evidence into visible work.

The third approach is the smallest complete and honest solution.

## Key Decisions

- Seed three fictional companies representing straight-through, exception-heavy, and multi-system
  handoff workflows.
- Generalize deterministic extraction only for transparent synthetic fixtures. Bedrock remains the
  required production provider.
- Store structured alpha assessments per evaluator, method, and perspective. Updates replace the
  evaluator's current assessment and retain audit history.
- Require a current seven-artifact packet before an AI-FDE assessment may claim completion.
- Derive lead time, claim yield, contradiction state, packet state, and token efficiency from
  canonical data instead of self-reported metrics.
- Do not calculate percentage improvement until at least three completed conventional and three
  completed AI-FDE operator assessments exist across three distinct workflows.
- Include assessments in engagement isolation, portability exports, export fingerprints, and
  permanent deletion.
- Tie deployed readiness evidence to the exact Git commit, immutable images, ECS rollback controls,
  and a completed Bedrock evaluation job for the configured model or inference profile.
- Keep sanitized data disabled unless the existing production go/no-go mechanism passes.

## Open Questions

No repository decision blocks implementation. External execution still requires named FDEs, an
Auth0 tenant, an AWS account, a Bedrock model or inference profile, and an approved design partner.
